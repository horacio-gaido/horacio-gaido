# Horacio Gaido

**Data Analyst · Business Intelligence · Process Automation**

Mendoza, Argentina · [Español](https://github.com/horacio-gaido/horacio-gaido/blob/main/README.md) | English

I am a data and Business Intelligence analyst. Since 2023 I have worked at Midas Consultores for YPF S.A., where I build ETL processes with Python and SQL Server, Power BI dashboards and Power Automate automations for nationwide IT service monitoring.

I came to data after more than 15 years in administration, finance and management control. That background lets me understand the business rules behind each indicator and talk with both business users and technical teams.

## What I do

- **ETL processes in Python.** Extraction, validation, transformation and loading with Pandas, NumPy and SQLAlchemy/pyodbc on SQL Server. I separate valid records from rejected ones, with the reason for each rejection, and keep configuration in JSON files.
- **Power BI dashboards.** Requirements gathering with users, data modeling, transformations in Power Query (M language), DAX measures and publishing to Power BI Service.
- **Service indicators.** KPIs built from ServiceNow tickets and reports split by region.
- **Automation.** Power Automate Cloud flows integrated with SharePoint, OneDrive and Outlook, Windows scheduled tasks, PowerShell and BAT scripts, and Selenium.
- **Data quality and traceability.** Controls and reconciliations before results are distributed, logs for every run and documentation of the rules applied.

> The work I do for YPF S.A. is confidential and is not published. The repositories on this profile are academic work and personal projects built on public data.
